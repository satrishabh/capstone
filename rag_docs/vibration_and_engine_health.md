# Synthetic Vehicle Service Guide — Engine Vibration

IMPORTANT: Synthetic educational content; not an OEM manual.

## Vibration guidance
- Below 5 mm/s: normal for this demo
- 5–7 mm/s: warning
- Above 7 mm/s: high

## Possible causes
- Engine mount deterioration
- Misfire
- Imbalance
- Rotating-component wear
- Bearing/internal engine wear
- Accessory or pulley problem

## Interpretation
Increasing vibration together with falling oil pressure can indicate mechanical degradation, but it does not prove an internal engine failure.

## Recommended checks
Review fault codes, engine balance/misfire data, mount condition, oil pressure, and mechanical measurements before authorizing major component replacement.
