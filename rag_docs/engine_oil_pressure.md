# Synthetic Vehicle Service Guide — Engine Oil Pressure

IMPORTANT: This is synthetic project documentation created for an educational predictive-maintenance demo.
It is NOT an OEM service manual and must not be used for real vehicle repair decisions.

## Normal operating guidance
For this synthetic diesel vehicle family:
- Normal warm-engine oil pressure: 2.0–4.5 bar
- 1.5–2.0 bar: warning; inspect and monitor
- Below 1.5 bar: critical; stop/restrict operation and inspect promptly

## Possible causes of low oil pressure
1. Low engine-oil level
2. Incorrect or degraded oil
3. Oil filter restriction
4. Oil pump degradation
5. Lubrication passage restriction
6. Internal engine/bearing wear
7. Faulty oil-pressure sensor or wiring

## Diagnostic sequence
1. Verify oil level.
2. Verify oil grade and oil condition.
3. Inspect for leaks.
4. Inspect/replace the oil filter if required.
5. Validate the oil-pressure sensor and wiring.
6. Measure mechanical oil pressure with an independent gauge.
7. Inspect the oil pump and lubrication system if pressure remains low.
8. Investigate internal wear only after simpler causes are excluded.

## Evidence interpretation
A single low reading should be verified. A persistent downward trend combined with increased vibration or temperature is a stronger predictive-maintenance signal.
