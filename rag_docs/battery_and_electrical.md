# Synthetic Vehicle Service Guide — Battery and Electrical System

IMPORTANT: Synthetic educational content; not an OEM manual.

## Battery-voltage guidance
- 12.4–12.8 V: healthy resting-voltage range for this demo
- 12.0–12.4 V: low
- Below 12.0 V: abnormal

A charging-system measurement while the engine is running should be interpreted separately from resting battery voltage.

## Possible causes of low voltage
- Weak battery
- Alternator/charging issue
- Electrical load
- Loose/corroded connection
- Sensor/power supply issue

Low battery voltage can create misleading sensor behavior, so electrical validation may be appropriate when several unrelated sensors become abnormal simultaneously.
